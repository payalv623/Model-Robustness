Target-guided SAM diagnostic

An approximate box and positive/negative points were manually marked on one training image. Candidates 0 and 1 recover the visible bird much better. Candidate 2 is incomplete. This diagnostic changes SAM's prompting mode; it is not a lowering of automatic quality thresholds and is not yet a production annotation strategy.

Next work

Provide class-targeted prompts through verified annotations or a validated grounding method, audit the retained object across all classes, then scale generation with cached masks and full output audits. No rejected pilot is a production dataset.

Nature and urban scenes differ in palette and structure, so this is a compound background-appearance shift rather than a purely semantic-context intervention. Neutral-gray and untouched-original controls are needed to measure segmentation artifacts separately.

The automatic pilot took roughly 22–60 seconds per image. Extrapolating its mean to all 13,500 images gives about 128 hours of segmentation alone, with substantial uncertainty. This is not an estimate for a different, prompted pipeline.
